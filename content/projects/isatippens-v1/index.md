## What it was

The first Svelte theme on this domain: a conventional portfolio — homepage, blog,
projects, gear — before the chassis, the faceplates and the portrait engine replaced it.

Kept because it is the control group. The content model (markdown in-repo, frontmatter
per entry, generated feed and sitemap) carried straight over into the rebuild; the
presentation is what changed.
