## What it is

A group assignment for COS 738, built on the premise of *Inception*: instead of dreaming
within dreams, augmented reality objects are placed on top of augmented reality.

## Requirements

- The Vuforia engine, installed in the Unity editor
  ([SDK](https://developer.vuforia.com/downloads/sdk)).
- A printed marker to anchor the first layer.
